Batteo Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-18

Batteo ("the app") is developed by TallTalha. This policy explains what the app does with data.

Summary

Batteo does not collect, sell or share personal data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. All battery readings and usage information stay on your phone.

Data the app processes on your device

  • Battery readings from Android (level, voltage, current, temperature, charge counter, health values reported by the system) and the app's own estimates.
  • Screen time and screen on/off periods per app, only if you grant "Usage access". Used to show which apps use the battery.
  • Settings you choose (theme, language, charge alarm, measurement options).

This data is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. It is not uploaded anywhere and is excluded from Android cloud backup. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

CSV export

If you export a measurement as a CSV file, the file is saved where you choose. What happens to that file afterwards is up to you.

Network use

The app does not send data over the internet. The optional in-app "wireless debugging pairing" connects only to your own phone (address 127.0.0.1) to grant the app permission to read the system battery health value.

Donations

Optional donations are processed by Google Play. Google handles the payment under its own privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). The app receives only the purchase result needed to thank you; it does not receive your payment details.

Permissions

  • Usage access, Modify system settings and Notifications are optional and requested only when you use the related feature.
  • A foreground service with a visible notification runs only while a measurement you started is in progress.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

Changes to this policy will be published on this page with a new date.
